Case Study: Corporation Business Analysis

Microsoft Excel

In this group project, I led the team in creating a detailed business and financial analysis of Costco to assess its overall business health and long‑term direction. We used Excel as our primary tool for compiling, organizing, and interpreting financial information, ensuring our analysis was both structured and data‑driven.

I coordinated group responsibilities, set timelines for research, and ensured that each section of the report aligned with our analytical goals and maintained a cohesive narrative.

This project strengthened my leadership, collaboration, and technical proficiency in Excel, in addition to my project management capabilities. It demonstrated my ability to guide a team through complex financial interpretation while producing a clear, organized, insight‑driven Excel workbook and analysis report.

Custom Professional Portfolio

Web Design & AI Collaboration
The portfolio you’re exploring right now began as a technical challenge I set for myself: to build a webpage from the ground up at zero cost while expanding my skill set. I designed and developed this site by leveraging AI tools, specifically Gemini, to bring my creative direction to life. Using a prompting approach known as "vibe coding", I guided Gemini with the aesthetic, tone, and structure I envisioned. From there, I refined the code with AI support, shaping the layout, colours, typography, and embedded files into a clean, personalized space that reflects my work and identity.

To publish the site and make it accessible through a custom URL, I used GitHub Pages as my hosting platform. Because the webpage was built from raw code rather than a template, I needed a way to store and serve the files publicly. GitHub provided the version control, file management, and static hosting required to display the site online at no cost. By uploading my project to a public repository and configuring GitHub Pages, I was able to connect my custom domain and ensure the site loads reliably for anyone who visits.

This project became a unique learning experience that pushed me to expand my technical abilities and adapt to new tools and workflows. It challenged me to think creatively, troubleshoot unfamiliar problems, and translate ideas into functional design. In the process, I strengthened my ability to collaborate with AI tools, refine technical outputs, and shape a polished final product that reflects both my skills and my curiosity.
